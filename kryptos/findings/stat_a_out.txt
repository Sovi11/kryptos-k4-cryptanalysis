--- 5. What IoC = 0.03608 actually licenses ------------------------
* Against the uniform-random null the observed value is at the 21.6th percentile.
  It is NOT 'anomalously flat'. It is an ordinary draw.
* The claim 'below uniform random, therefore the masking is visible' is
  unsupported: 21.6% of random 97-letter strings are at least this low, and
  the gap (0.00238) is 0.84 null standard deviations.
* What the IoC DOES exclude, at overwhelming significance, is any system
  that leaves an English-sized IoC: pure transposition (K3's own ciphertext
  sits at IoC 0.0662) and short-period polyalphabetics.
