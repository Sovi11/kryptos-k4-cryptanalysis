--- 5. OMNIBUS: is there ANY detectable structure in K4? ------------
Every individual K4 'anomaly' in the literature is one statistic picked
after the fact from a battery.  The honest question is: how surprising is
the SMALLEST of the battery's p-values?  Null = one-time pad over English,
which is exactly a uniform-random 97-letter string.

  battery of 7 statistics, 40,000 null replicates
  statistic      K4 value   two-sided p vs uniform
  ioc              0.0361                   0.4381
  distinct        26.0000                   1.0000
  doubles          6.0000                   0.3304
  maxz             3.0358                   0.4312
  maxperz          1.1285                   0.1309
  bigrams         10.0000                   0.2928
  trigrams         0.0000                   1.0000

  K4's smallest p over the battery: 0.1309  (statistic maxperz)
  null distribution of that minimum: median 0.1697, 5th pct 0.0140
  OMNIBUS p = P(min-p <= K4's min-p | uniform) = 0.4142

  (The marginal CDFs are estimated from the same sample that supplies the
   null replicates, which biases the omnibus p DOWNWARD -- i.e. in favour
   of finding structure.  Even so it is nowhere near significance.
   The one statistic not in this battery is the width-21 column-bigram
   count; stat_e_kasiski.py shows its multiple-testing-corrected p is 0.2-0.4,
   so adding it could only raise this omnibus p, never lower it.)

  CONCLUSION: no statistic in the classical battery detects any structure
  in K4 once selection is paid for.  K4 is, at n = 97, statistically
  indistinguishable from a uniformly random string of letters.
