F1. CLASSIC ADFGVX AND ADFGX ARE EXCLUDED OUTRIGHT
    ADFGVX ciphertext is written in the six letters A D F G V X (ADFGX: five,
    A D F G X).  Two independent counts kill it:
      (1) alphabet.  K4 uses 26 distinct letters, all 26 of them:
          A4 B5 C2 D3 E2 F4 G4 H2 I4 J3 K8 L4 M1 N3 O5 P3 Q4 R4 S6 T6 U6 V2 W5 X2 Y1 Z4
          Letters outside {A,D,F,G,V,X} in K4: 78 of 97 (80%).
      (2) parity.  ADFGVX emits exactly two symbols per plaintext letter, so
          its ciphertext length is EVEN.  K4 is 97 letters, odd.  Even with a
          hypothetical 26-symbol coordinate alphabet the length could not be 97.
    Both hold for every square, every transposition key and every key width,
    so no search is required.

F3. THE COUNTING BOUNDARY FOR WIDE OR ARBITRARY TRANSPOSITIONS
    Historical ADFGVX keys were 15-22 letters wide; 15! = 1.31e+12 and 22! = 1.12e+21
    are beyond enumeration, and an ARBITRARY permutation of the 194-symbol
    coordinate stream has 194! = 10^361 members.
    Information check: the cribs carry 24 * log2(26) = 113 bits.
    A 6x6 square carries log2(36!) = 138 bits; a width-15 columnar key
    log2(15!) = 40 bits; a free permutation log2(194!) = 1200 bits.
    Key entropy exceeds the crib evidence by a factor of 2 even in the
    narrowest historical case, so consistent (square, key) pairs are
    GUARANTEED to exist by counting alone and finding one proves nothing.
    Wide-key ADFGVX is therefore not falsifiable from the cribs -- the same
    boundary matrix_e_rotor.py documents for the M-209 and SIGABA.  What IS
    settled: classic ADFGVX/ADFGX (F1), and every narrow key, width 2-7 (F2).
