  97 is prime, so the ONLY exact rectangles for 97 letters are 1x97 and 97x1: no
  columnar transposition of the whole of K4 has a full last row, and no square or
  near-square grid exists.  Every 97-letter grid is ragged, which is why the
  sculpture sets OBKR aside: 97 - 4 = 93 = 3 x 31 is exact, and 31 is the panel width.
  98 = 2 x 7^2 = 7 x 14; 96 = 2^5 x 3.

5. 97 +/- 1: A MISSING OR EXTRA CARVED LETTER
==============================================================================
  K2's carved ciphertext is one letter short of its plaintext (Sanborn's error),
  so the same could be true of K4.  Insert an unknown letter at slot s (0 = before
  OBKR, which is where the "?" sits and would make K4 98 = 7 x 14 characters);
  every crib at a position > s moves up by one.  Also delete each non-crib letter.
  Only the crib POSITIONS matter for the consistency test, so the inserted letter
  being unknown costs nothing.
