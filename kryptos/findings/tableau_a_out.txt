## 2. Shape of the carved tableau

28 carved rows.  Row 0 and row 27 are the unlabelled plain header/footer
  'ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ' + 'ABCD' = 30 letters.
Rows 1..26 are 'row label' + a 30-letter window of the KRYPTOS-keyed alphabet:
  row with label L(i) carries KEYED rotated left by i, then its first 4 letters again.
row lengths (physical): [30, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 32, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 30]
row lengths (ideal)   : [30, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31, 30]
total letters physical = 867, ideal = 866, difference = 1
  866 = 2*30 (header+footer) + 26*(1 label + 30 body).

## 3. Complete anomaly inventory (physical minus ideal)

rows that differ from the ideal tableau: 1
  row index 14 (carved label 'N', 1-indexed carved row 15):
    physical 'NGHIJLMNQUVWXZKRYPTOSABCDEFGHIJL'  (32 chars)
    ideal    'NGHIJLMNQUVWXZKRYPTOSABCDEFGHIJ'  (31 chars)
    first divergence at column 31 (0-indexed within the row)
    physical row = ideal row + one extra 'L' appended at the end

There is exactly ONE deviation: a single extra letter L at the end of the row
labelled N (carved row 15 of 28, 1-indexed).  Everything else is a perfect
KRYPTOS-keyed Vigenere tableau.

linear index of the extra L in the 867-letter physical reading:
  0-indexed 464, 1-indexed 465
  letters before it: 464 ; letters after it: 402
  factorisations of interest: 465 = 3*5*31 = 15*31 ; 464 = 16*29 ; 867 = 3*17^2
