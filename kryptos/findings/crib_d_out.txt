TASK (d)  PART 1: what a misspelling-tolerant score actually tolerates
==============================================================================
q_tolD = mean quadgram log10 probability after discarding the D worst
quadgrams.  One wrong letter damages at most 4 quadgrams, so D=8
absorbs two deliberate misspellings and D=16 absorbs four.

  97-letter text                                    q     q_tol8    q_tol16
  English, 0 random letters corrupted          -4.972     -4.670     -4.438
  English, 1 random letters corrupted          -5.053     -4.744     -4.487
  English, 2 random letters corrupted          -5.144     -4.839     -4.549
  English, 3 random letters corrupted          -5.225     -4.923     -4.622
  English, 5 random letters corrupted          -5.391     -5.105     -4.780
