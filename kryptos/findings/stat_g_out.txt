--- The conclusion that matters ------------------------------------
At 97 letters the classical statistics are blind beyond a key length of
about 8.  Every negative in this project that rests on
statistics alone therefore applies ONLY to the short-key end of the space.
Periods 27, 28, 29 and everything >= 53 -- the ones the cribs cannot
constrain either -- are exactly the region where BOTH tools are powerless.
That region is not 'ruled out by statistics'; it is unexamined.
