     ... CONDITIONAL on exactly 10 gaps            7               0.20487   [17,987 of 500,000 replicates had 10 gaps]

  (K4's best width for column bigrams is 21, with 11.)

  READING, in two parts.
  (i) CONVENTION.  K4 scores 11 under BOTH conventions, because no vertical
      bigram occurs three times at width 21.  The NULLS differ: a triple
      contributes 3 to the pairs count but only 2 to the excess count, so the
      pairs statistic has the heavier upper tail.  Bean's published ~1/6750
      is the excess convention; this repo's earlier prior_art_bean_stats.py
      measured 1/3226 for it.  The pairs convention gives ~1/650 on the same
      null.  Same observation, same data, a factor of ~5 in nominal
      significance, decided by an unstated choice.
  (ii) SELECTION.  Width 21 was chosen from among all plausible widths. Once
      the MAXIMUM over widths 2..48 is scored against its own null, the
      effect is what scanning 47 widths always produces. It is the strongest
      single anomaly in K4 and it does not survive its own correction under
      either convention.
